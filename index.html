Terms of Engagement & Retainer Governance

Clear, transparent guidelines designed to protect both the client and the studio. No ambiguity, no surprises.

1. 100% Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon final invoice clearance, 100% of the custom codebase, repository rights, custom assets, and deployment access belong entirely to you. You are never locked into our systems, and can host wherever you choose.

2. Revision Rounds & Scope Architecture

Every development build includes two structured milestone revisions (Wireframe/Architecture phase & Final Pre-Launch polish). Any major architectural pivot outside initial agreed briefs is scoped as an add-on sprint.

3. Retainer Service Level Agreement (SLA)

For clients on our monthly retainers ($299, $499, $899): routine content requests are deployed within 24 to 48 hours. Critical infrastructure outages receive sub-1-hour emergency response prioritization.

4. Retainer Cancellation & Invoicing

All monthly retainers operate on a month-to-month basis with zero lock-in contracts. You may pause or terminate the agreement anytime with a simple 14-day notice prior to the subsequent billing cycle.

5. Transparent Hosting & Zero Markup

We deploy on industry-standard cloud providers (Vercel, Cloudflare Edge, AWS). Your domain and direct cloud accounts remain in your name, ensuring absolute operational control.

6. Mutual Confidentiality (NDA Standard)

All proprietary business figures, backend database logic, API credentials, and trade data shared with arweb_ studio are legally protected under our mutual non-disclosure policy.

Frequently Answered Questions

How fast can my website be designed and deployed?

Our single-page $499 Launchpad typically ships within 4 to 6 business days. The $999 Studio Flagship multi-page platform ships in 10 to 14 days after receiving initial content and brand assets.

Why should I choose custom engineering over WordPress or Wix?

WordPress and drag-and-drop builders load dozens of bloated scripts, plugins, and databases that make websites slow, insecure, and vulnerable to hacks. We write pure, high-performance code that achieves 100/100 Google Core Web Vitals, ranks higher in SEO, and loads in less than 500 milliseconds.

Do I have to pay monthly fees if I only purchase a website build?

No. All our website development builds ($499, $999, $1,899+) are 100% one-time flat investments. Monthly care retainers ($299/mo) are completely optional for clients who want us to actively manage their ongoing updates, SEO, and cloud infrastructure.

What is included in the intellectual property handoff?

You receive full Git repository access, compiled static builds, design source files, domain DNS routing configurations, and SSL certificate documentation. You have 100% full ownership forever.
