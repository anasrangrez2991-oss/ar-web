Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we build.

How long does a website take to build?

A 1-page sprint goes live in 48 hours. A multi-page business website takes 5 to 7 days from kick-off to final testing. You get daily WhatsApp updates throughout.

Can I start with a 1-page site and upgrade to multi-pages later?

Yes, absolutely. We architect all our builds modularly so you can easily expand with new service pages, case studies, or blogs as your business grows.

Are there any hidden monthly agency fees?

Zero. You pay a transparent one-time project fee. Hosting is free for the first year, and you have 100% full intellectual property ownership of your website and code.

Will my website be fast on mobile phones?

Every website we engineer achieves 98+ PageSpeed scores on Google Lighthouse and loads in under 1 second worldwide via Vercel Edge networks.
