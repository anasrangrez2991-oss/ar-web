Studio Governance & Legal Standards

Terms of Service, Governance & SLA

Transparent contractual standards governing all bespoke digital builds and continuous management retainers commissioned through arweb_.

Clause 01

100% Intellectual Property & Source Code Ownership

Upon clearance of final milestone payments, complete legal ownership of all bespoke source code, markup repositories, custom design assets, and database configurations is transferred to the client. The client is never bound by proprietary studio locks or licensing royalties.

Clause 02

Scope Architecture & Milestone Approvals

Engagements are governed by a strictly defined project brief. Build stages proceed through Wireframe Proof, Bespoke Design Interface, Edge Engineering, and Deployment Testing. Fundamental structural changes outside the agreed sprint scope will be separately estimated and billed.

Clause 03

Service Level Agreement (SLA) for Retainers

Subscribers to our monthly management retainers ($299, $499, $899/month) receive direct access to Anas Rangrez. Routine content updates and performance audits are fulfilled within 24 to 48 business hours. Critical production downtime issues receive emergency response within under 1 hour.

Clause 04

Retainer Invoicing & Cancellation Policy

All maintenance and retainer subscriptions are billed monthly in advance and operate on an open, month-to-month commitment. Retainers may be paused or terminated without penalty upon 14 days written notice prior to the beginning of the next recurring billing cycle.

Clause 05

Speed SLA Benchmark & Guarantee

Every digital flagship delivered by arweb_ is contractually guaranteed to achieve 95+ to 100/100 Core Web Vitals on desktop and mobile upon initial deployment on edge infrastructure. We do not approve builds with unoptimized rendering pipelines.

Clause 06

Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

All client strategic documentation, proprietary brand data, unreleased product blueprints, and technical architecture shared with arweb_ remain under strict mutual confidentiality throughout and following project completion.

Clause 07

Third-Party SaaS & Infrastructure Accounts

Hosting accounts (Cloudflare, Vercel, AWS), custom domains, and CRM/payment gateways (Stripe, Resend) are registered directly in the client's legal entity name. arweb_ configures and maintains the systems but never holds client credentials hostage.

Clause 08

Post-Launch Warranty Period

All bespoke builds include a complimentary 14-day post-launch warranty covering bug remediation, browser rendering discrepancies, and DNS configuration adjustments to ensure seamless live operations.
